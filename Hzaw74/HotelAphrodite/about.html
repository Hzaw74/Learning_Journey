About Hotel Aphrodite

Founded in 1995, Hotel Aphrodite was born from a vision to create a sanctuary where luxury meets comfort. Named after the Greek goddess of beauty, our hotel embodies elegance in every detail. From our meticulously designed rooms to our world-class dining, every aspect of your stay is curated to provide an unforgettable experience.

Located in the heart of Paradise City, we are just steps away from the city's finest attractions, yet tucked away enough to offer a peaceful retreat.
